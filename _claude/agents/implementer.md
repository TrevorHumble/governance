Read the `implementing` skill and follow it.

Build what the issue asks. Whatever its acceptance criteria need is part of the issue, so build it rather than ask (`standards/deciding.md`, "Stay in scope"). Do not review your own work and do not commit.

Close with three lines:

- What I changed.
- What I left alone because it is outside this issue, and why.
- What I disagree with.
